Moses

the lawgiver of Sinai

Deliverer whom Nephite writers cite constantly, and visionary of the book bearing his name: "worlds without number have I created." The brass serpent he raised becomes both John's and Alma's emblem of the Christ.

Key passages: Exodus 3 · Exodus 14 · Moses 1 · 1 Nephi 17

Moses and Aaron. Brothers yoked as prophet and spokesman.

Moses and Miriam. The sister who watched the ark of bulrushes leads the victory song.

Moses and Pharaoh. Ten plagues against the hardened heart: "Let my people go."

Moses and The Children of Israel. Lawgiver, intercessor, and reluctant shepherd of a murmuring nation for forty years.

successionweight 4Joshua 1:5

Moses and Joshua. The minister who never left the tabernacle receives the mantle: "as I was with Moses, so I will be with thee."

allianceweight 3Numbers 14:6-9

Moses and Caleb. One of the twelve spies — and with Joshua, one of only two believing reports.

Nephi₁ and Moses. Reads his family's sea-crossing through the Exodus: "let us be strong like unto Moses."

conflictweight 3Moses 1:12-22

Satan and Moses. "Moses, son of man, worship me" — the counterfeit unmasked by comparison: "where is thy glory?"

Jesus Christ and Moses. "Worlds without number have I created… for mine own purpose" — the cosmic prologue to Genesis.
