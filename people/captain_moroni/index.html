Rulers and judges · Book of Mormon

Moroni₁

the captain — chief commander of the Nephite armies (not Moroni₂ son of Mormon)

Rends his coat into the title of liberty; Mormon's verdict on him is the record's highest praise of any mortal: "if all men had been… like unto Moroni, behold, the very powers of hell would have been shaken forever."

Key passages: Alma 46 · Alma 48 · Alma 60
