Moroni₂

son of Mormon — last Nephite and final custodian of the plates (not the captain)

"I even remain alone." Wanders twenty years past Cumorah sealing the record with a promise to its future reader — and returns, fourteen centuries later, as the glorious messenger of Joseph Smith's history to point out where he buried it.

Key passages: Mormon 8 · Moroni 10 · Joseph Smith--History 1

On the timeline

AD 385Mormon 6

The last battle at Cumorah. "O ye fair ones" — the Nephite nation ends; Mormon falls with it.

AD 421Moroni 10

Moroni seals the record. Alone for decades, he closes with a promise addressed to the future reader — then buries the plates.

AD 1823Joseph Smith--History 1

Moroni's night visits. The Book of Mormon's final historian, fourteen centuries on, appears three times in one night quoting Malachi, Isaiah, and Joel.

AD 1827Joseph Smith--History 1

The plates entrusted. After four annual visits to the hill, the record Moroni buried changes hands.
