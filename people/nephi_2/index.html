Prophets and seers · Book of Mormon

Nephi₂

son of Helaman₃ — prophet given the sealing power (not the founding Nephi₁)

Prays from his garden tower, exposes a judge's murder mid-sermon, and receives the words "all things shall be done unto thee according to thy word" — then asks not for victory but famine, to spare the people from war.

Key passages: Helaman 7 · Helaman 10 · Helaman 11
