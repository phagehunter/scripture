Isaiah

son of Amoz, prophet of Jerusalem

The most quoted prophet in the canon — twenty-one full chapters are woven into the Book of Mormon, and the risen Christ commands: "great are the words of Isaiah."

Key passages: Isaiah 6 · Isaiah 53 · 2 Nephi 12

Relationships (8)

allianceweight 3Isaiah 38:1-8

Isaiah and Hezekiah. Prophet and king together face Assyria; fifteen years added by the shadow's retreat.

divine encounterweight 4Isaiah 6:1-8

Jesus Christ and Isaiah. The temple vision: the seraphim, the coal, and "whom shall I send?"

seen in visionweight 3Isaiah 44:28

Isaiah and Cyrus. Named generations early — "my shepherd," even "his anointed" — an outsider drafted by prophecy.

quotes or expoundsweight 3Acts 8:30-35

Philip the Evangelist and Isaiah. From the Gaza-road chariot, Isaiah 53 opened: "began at the same scripture, and preached unto him Jesus."

quotes or expoundsweight 51 Nephi 19:23

Nephi₁ and Isaiah. Copies whole chapters onto his plates "that I might more fully persuade them to believe in the Lord their Redeemer."

quotes or expoundsweight 32 Nephi 6:4-5

Jacob₂ and Isaiah. Preaches the covenant chapters (Isaiah 50–51) to the Nephites at Nephi's request.

quotes or expoundsweight 4Mosiah 14:1-12

Abinadi and Isaiah. Recites Isaiah 53 whole to the court: "who shall declare his generation?"

quotes or expoundsweight 43 Nephi 23:1-3

Jesus Christ and Isaiah. "Great are the words of Isaiah" — the risen Christ quotes Isaiah 54 entire and commands the people to search him.
