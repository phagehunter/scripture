Melchizedek

king of Salem, priest of the Most High God

Appears for three verses with bread, wine, and a blessing — and echoes forever: Psalm 110's eternal priesthood, Hebrews' order of the Son, and Alma's sermon on the priest-king "than whom none were greater."

Key passages: Genesis 14 · Hebrews 7 · Alma 13
