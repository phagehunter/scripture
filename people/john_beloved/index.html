John the Beloved

son of Zebedee, the disciple Jesus loved

Evangelist of the Word made flesh and seer of Patmos; Nephi₁ is shown him in vision as the appointed writer of "the remainder of these things" — and the Three Nephites are granted his same tarrying wish.

Key passages: John 13 · Revelation 1 · 1 Nephi 14 · 3 Nephi 28
