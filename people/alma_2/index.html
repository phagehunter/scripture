Prophets and seers · Book of Mormon

Alma₂

the younger — son of Alma₁; first chief judge and high priest

Struck down mid-rebellion by an angel (the book's Damascus road); resigns the judgment-seat to preach, authors the seed-of-faith discourse, and departs the record as mysteriously as Moses.

Key passages: Mosiah 27 · Alma 5 · Alma 32 · Alma 36
