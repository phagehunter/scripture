Priscilla

wife of Aquila — tentmaker and teacher

Named, unusually, before her husband more often than after: co-instructor of the eloquent Apollos and host of a house-church Paul thanks "in Christ Jesus."

Key passages: Acts 18 · Romans 16

Priscilla and Aquila. The tentmaking couple, named always together — six times in the New Testament.

Paul and Priscilla. Same craft, shared roof, and a house-church greeting: "my helpers in Christ Jesus, who have for my life laid down their own necks."
