Jacob (Israel)

son of Isaac — the patriarch, not Jacob₂ son of Lehi

Supplanter, ladder-dreamer, wrestler at Peniel; his twelve sons become the tribes whose scattering and gathering is the master-plot of all three volumes.

Key passages: Genesis 28 · Genesis 32
