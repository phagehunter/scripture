Zenos

brass-plates prophet known only through the Book of Mormon

An Old-World prophet preserved solely on the plates of brass: his sprawling allegory of the tame and wild olive trees is the longest single parable in the canon.

Key passages: Jacob 5 · Alma 33 · Helaman 8
