Disciples and missionaries · Book of Mormon

Ammon₂

son of Mosiah₂ — missionary to King Lamoni (not the Mulekite explorer)

Renounces a kingdom to serve a Lamanite king as shepherd; the severed arms at the waters of Sebus open a door that fourteen years of preaching walk through.

Key passages: Alma 17 · Alma 18 · Alma 26
