Peoples and movements · Book of Mormon

The People of Zarahemla

Mulekites — descendants of Zedekiah's son Mulek

A second, recordless Jerusalem exodus discovered by Mosiah₁ — more numerous than the Nephites, their corrupted language the book's case study in what happens without scripture.

A collective node standing for about 100,000 people.

Key passages: Omni 1 · Mosiah 25 · Helaman 8
