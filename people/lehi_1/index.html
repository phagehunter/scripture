Lehi₁

founding patriarch — father of Nephi₁ (three later Lehis follow)

A Jerusalem contemporary of Jeremiah who dreams of the tree of life and leads his quarreling family across the sea — the Book of Mormon's Abraham.

Key passages: 1 Nephi 1 · 1 Nephi 8 · 2 Nephi 2

Open Lehi₁ in the network

On the timeline

600 BC1 Nephi 2

Lehi leaves Jerusalem. First year of Zedekiah: the family flees before the destruction Lehi prophesied.

589 BC1 Nephi 18

Arrival in the promised land. Eight years in Arabia, then the ship Nephi was shown how to build.
