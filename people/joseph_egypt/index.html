Joseph of Egypt

son of Jacob — not Joseph₂ son of Lehi, nor Joseph of Nazareth

Dreamer, slave, vizier, and reconciler. The Book of Mormon presents Lehi's family as his literal branch, "a branch broken off," and preserves prophecies attributed to him.

Key passages: Genesis 37 · Genesis 45 · 2 Nephi 3
