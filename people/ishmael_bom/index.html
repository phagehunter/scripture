Ishmael

Jerusalem patriarch who joined Lehi (not Abraham's son)

His household of daughters makes the colony a people rather than a family; buried at Nahom, where his daughters' mourning nearly turns the camp back.

Key passages: 1 Nephi 7 · 1 Nephi 16
