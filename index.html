Scripture Atlas

A visual literary companion to the King James Bible, the Book of Mormon and the Pearl of Great Price, by Curtis Hoffmann

This dashboard reads three volumes of scripture at a distance and up close at once: visualizations built from 192 curated figures and collectives, 320 cited relationships, 63 annotated cross-references, a reference web of 7,052 verse pairs and a two-hemisphere chronology, beside a reader with the complete 38,341-verse text of all 86 books.

Use the volume chips, the twelve-era window and the seven role-group toggles to filter every view, and press play to watch the network assemble era by era. Search any figure; homonyms carry subscript numbers (Alma₁ and Alma₂, Nephi₁ to Nephi₃) so they are never confused, and focus mode isolates one figure's network. Clicking a node, edge or arc opens the commentary panel, and every citation links into the Text tab, where a name resolves to the right person for its chapter.

What the three views show

  • Character Network. A force-directed graph with node size by degree centrality, color by role group and eight typed edge classes: family, succession, teaching, conflict, alliance, divine encounter, vision and quotation. Cross-volume edges are the connective tissue, from Nephi₁ seeing Mary in vision to Moroni₂ returning as the messenger of Joseph Smith's history.
  • Cross-References. A web of 7,052 verse pairs: 519 from the verse-aligned quotation blocks (Isaiah, Malachi and Matthew in the Book of Mormon) and 6,533 from the dataset's Bible / Book of Mormon reference listing, which is mechanical and generous and is labeled as such; aggregated into 532 book-to-book arcs with a threshold control, plus 63 annotated highlights with commentary. Any pair opens side by side in the Compare tab, and references appear verse by verse in the reader.
  • Parallel Chronology. Old World and New World on one zoomable time axis: the Jaredites beside the patriarchs, Lehi departing during Jeremiah's ministry, the darkness of AD 33 marked in both hemispheres, and the Restoration era of the Pearl of Great Price.

Read without the app

Everything here also exists as a plain page: all 86 books with their full text, figures and cross-references, 192 people with every cited relationship, the annotated cross-references, and the parallel chronology.

Text: the LDS Scriptures dataset (scriptures.nephi.org, 2020-12-08 edition), which publishes the King James Bible, the Book of Mormon and the Pearl of Great Price as public domain. Characters, relationships, cross-references and dates are hand-curated; Book of Mormon dating follows the text's internal year counts, and Old World dates before the monarchy are flagged as traditional.
