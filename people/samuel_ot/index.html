Samuel

prophet and last judge (not Samuel the Lamanite)

"Speak, for thy servant heareth." Kingmaker and king-breaker, anointing both Saul and David.

Key passages: 1 Samuel 3 · 1 Samuel 16

On the timeline

1050 BC1 Samuel 10

Samuel anoints Saul. The last judge inaugurates the monarchy he warned against.

Relationships (5)

familyweight 41 Samuel 1:27-28

Hannah and Samuel. The son prayed for at Shiloh and lent back to the Lord "as long as he liveth."

teaching and conversionweight 31 Samuel 3:8-10

Eli and Samuel. Teaches the boy the answer to the night voice: "Speak, Lord; for thy servant heareth."

divine encounterweight 31 Samuel 3:19-20

Jesus Christ and Samuel. The call by name in the sanctuary dark — Israel learns "that Samuel was established to be a prophet."

successionweight 41 Samuel 15:22-23

Samuel and Saul. Anoints Israel's first king — then delivers the rejection: "to obey is better than sacrifice."

successionweight 41 Samuel 16:7-13

Samuel and David. The horn of oil at Bethlehem, passing over seven brothers: "man looketh on the outward appearance, but the Lord looketh on the heart."
