Nebuchadnezzar

king of Babylon

Destroyer of Jerusalem and dreamer of the image with feet of clay — the empire personified, humbled to eat grass.

Key passages: Daniel 2 · Daniel 4 · 2 Kings 25

On the timeline

586 BC2 Kings 25

Jerusalem falls to Babylon. The destruction Lehi prophesied and fled; Mulek's escape party also departs (per Helaman 8:21).

Nebuchadnezzar and Daniel. The dream no magician could repeat, interpreted by the captive: the image with feet of clay.

Nebuchadnezzar and Belshazzar. "Father" in the chapter's court idiom — the predecessor whose humbling Belshazzar knew and ignored.
