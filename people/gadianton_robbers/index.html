The Gadianton Robbers

the oath-bound secret combination

Murder sworn to secrecy and secrecy sworn to power: the underground society that migrates from city to wilderness and back until it wears the government itself.

A collective node standing for about 30,000 people.

Key passages: Helaman 6 · 3 Nephi 3 · 3 Nephi 4
