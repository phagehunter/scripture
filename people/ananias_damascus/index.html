Ananias

disciple at Damascus who healed Saul (not the husband of Sapphira)

Ordered to lay hands on the church's most feared enemy, he objects once, obeys, and opens the apostle's eyes with two words: "Brother Saul."

Key passages: Acts 9 · Acts 22
