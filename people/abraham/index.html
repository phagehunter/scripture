Abraham

father of the covenant

Called out of Ur with a promise numbering his seed as the stars — the covenant the Book of Mormon claims for Lehi's branch, the New Testament universalizes, and the book bearing his own name traces back before the world was.

Key passages: Genesis 12 · Genesis 22 · Abraham 3 · 1 Nephi 15
