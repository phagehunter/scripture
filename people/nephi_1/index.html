Prophets and seers · Book of Mormon

Nephi₁

son of Lehi₁ — founding prophet-king and first record-keeper

"I will go and do." Bow-breaker, ship-builder, Isaiah-quoter, and reluctant slayer of Laban; his small plates open the book and his name becomes the royal title of his people.

Key passages: 1 Nephi 3 · 1 Nephi 11 · 2 Nephi 33

Open Nephi₁ in the network

On the timeline

600 BC1 Nephi 2

Lehi leaves Jerusalem. First year of Zedekiah: the family flees before the destruction Lehi prophesied.

597 BC1 Nephi 11

Nephi's vision of the tree. The condescension of God: Mary, the Baptist, the Twelve, and John — seen six centuries early.

589 BC1 Nephi 18

Arrival in the promised land. Eight years in Arabia, then the ship Nephi was shown how to build.

570 BC2 Nephi 5

Nephite–Lamanite division. After Lehi's death the family fractures permanently into two nations.
