Philip the Evangelist

deacon of Acts — not the apostle

One of the seven; takes the gospel to Samaria and, on the Gaza road, expounds Isaiah 53 from a chariot seat to the Ethiopian treasurer.

Key passages: Acts 8 · Acts 21
