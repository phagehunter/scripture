The Zoramites

apostate sect of the Rameumptom

Weekly they mount their tower to thank God they are elect, then think of him no more — Alma₂'s mission to them yields the poor in heart and the seed-of-faith sermon.

A collective node standing for about 20,000 people.

Key passages: Alma 31 · Alma 32
