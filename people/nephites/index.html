Peoples and movements · Book of Mormon

The Nephites

the record-keeping nation descended from Nephi₁

The covenant civilization whose thousand-year arc — founding, pride cycles, golden age, extinction — is the Book of Mormon's tragic spine.

A collective node standing for about 300,000 people.

Key passages: 2 Nephi 5 · 4 Nephi 1 · Mormon 6

570 BC2 Nephi 5

Nephite–Lamanite division. After Lehi's death the family fractures permanently into two nations.

The Gadianton Robbers and The Nephites. From hidden band to shadow government: "they did obtain the sole management of the government."

Jesus Christ and The Nephites. The descent at the temple: 2,500 people feel the wounds one by one — "arise and come forth unto me."

Mormon and The Nephites. General at sixteen, refusing and resuming command of a doomed people he cannot stop loving.
