Rulers and judges · Book of Mormon

Gideon

Nephite patriot (not the judge of Israel)

Drives King Noah to his tower sword in hand, engineers Limhi's escape — and in old age is cut down unarmed by Nehor, the judges' first martyr.

Key passages: Mosiah 19 · Mosiah 22 · Alma 1
