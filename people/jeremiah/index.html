Jeremiah

prophet of the fall of Jerusalem

Weeps over the city he cannot save; Nephi₁ cites his imprisonment as proof of the Jerusalem Lehi fled, and his scattered-remnant themes saturate Nephite prophecy.

Key passages: Jeremiah 1 · Jeremiah 38 · 1 Nephi 7
