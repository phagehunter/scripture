Prophets and seers · Book of Mormon

Helaman₂

son of Alma₂ — record-keeper and leader of the two thousand (Helaman₁ was Benjamin's son)

Receives his father's counsel and the sacred records, then fathers no army but leads one: the two thousand sons of converted Lamanites who "never had fought, yet… did not fear death."

Key passages: Alma 36 · Alma 56 · Alma 57
