Rulers and judges · Book of Mormon

Mosiah₂

son of Benjamin — last Nephite king, translator, founder of the judges

Seer who translates the Jaredite plates, sends his sons to the Lamanites, and abolishes his own throne in favor of elected judges — the book's constitutional moment.

Key passages: Mosiah 6 · Mosiah 28 · Mosiah 29
