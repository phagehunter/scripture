Peoples and movements · Book of Mormon

The Lamanites

the rival nation descended from Laman₁ and Lemuel

Cast first as antagonists, repeatedly revealed as the more redeemable: converted by thousands, once more righteous than the Nephites — and the surviving remnant to whom the book is addressed.

A collective node standing for about 500,000 people.

Key passages: 2 Nephi 5 · Helaman 6 · Mormon 7

Amalickiah and The Lamanites. By poison, forgery, and murder he seizes the Lamanite throne to spend it on revenge.

Nephi₂ and The Lamanites. With Lehi₄ he baptizes thousands of Lamanites — who then surpass the Nephites in righteousness.

Mormon and The Lamanites. The final wars: Cumorah's quarter-million dead and the lament, "O ye fair ones."
