Prophets and seers · Book of Mormon

Nephi₃

son of Nephi₂ — disciple who chronicled the signs and met the risen Christ

Prays all day as the deadline for the believers' execution nears and hears the voice: "on the morrow come I into the world." Raises his brother from the dead; first of the New-World Twelve.

Key passages: 3 Nephi 1 · 3 Nephi 7 · 3 Nephi 19

On the timeline

AD 13 Nephi 1

The sign of Christ's birth. Night without darkness, on the eve of the believers' execution.

AD 34.53 Nephi 11

Christ at the temple in Bountiful. 2,500 people feel the wounds one by one; the Sermon redelivered; the sacrament instituted.
