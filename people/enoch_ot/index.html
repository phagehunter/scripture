Enoch

seventh from Adam, who "walked with God"

Five verses in Genesis, two chapters in the book of Moses: the seer who builds a city so righteous it is taken up whole, and who watches the God of heaven weep over the residue.

Key passages: Genesis 5 · Moses 6 · Moses 7
