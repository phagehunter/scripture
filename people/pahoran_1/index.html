Rulers and judges · Book of Mormon

Pahoran₁

third chief judge, during the great war

Falsely accused of treason in Moroni₁'s angriest letter, he answers without offense: "in your epistle you have censured me, but it mattereth not; I am not angry, but do rejoice in the greatness of your heart."

Key passages: Alma 50 · Alma 61
