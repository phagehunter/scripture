Prophets and seers · Book of Mormon

Mormon

prophet-general and abridger; the book bears his name

General at sixteen, historian of a dying nation, editor of a thousand years of plates. His letters to his son and his lament over the slain — "O ye fair ones" — close the record's human register.

Key passages: Mormon 1 · Mormon 6 · Words of Mormon 1 · Moroni 8
