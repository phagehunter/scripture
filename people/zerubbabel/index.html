Zerubbabel

Davidic governor who rebuilt the temple

Heir of the deposed royal line, he lays the second temple's foundation while old men weep for the first — and carries the genealogy onward toward Joseph of Nazareth.

Key passages: Ezra 3 · Haggai 2 · Matthew 1
