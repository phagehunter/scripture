Old World

2100 BCGenesis 12AbrahamSarah

Abraham's call & covenant. Traditional dating (~2100–1900 BC): the covenant of land, seed, and universal blessing.

1900 BCGenesis 32Jacob (Israel)

Jacob becomes Israel. The night-wrestle at Peniel; twelve sons become twelve tribes.

1850 BCGenesis 41Joseph of Egypt

Joseph in Egypt. Sold, imprisoned, exalted; Israel descends into Egypt in famine.

1450 BCExodus 14MosesAaronMiriamThe Children of Israel

The Exodus. Traditional early date (~1446 BC): deliverance through the sea, covenant at Sinai.

1400 BCJoshua 6Joshua

Conquest of Canaan. Jordan crossed, Jericho fallen, land allotted.

1200 BCJudges 2DeborahGideonSamson

Age of the Judges. The cycle: apostasy, oppression, cry, deliverer — repeated for two centuries.

1100 BCRuth 4RuthNaomiBoaz

Ruth & the Bethlehem line. A Moabite convert becomes great-grandmother of David.

1050 BC1 Samuel 10SamuelSaul

Samuel anoints Saul. The last judge inaugurates the monarchy he warned against.

1010 BC2 Samuel 5David

David's reign. Jerusalem taken; the dynastic covenant given through Nathan.

966 BC1 Kings 8Solomon

Solomon's temple. The temple Nephi₁ later says his own New-World temple followed in form.

930 BC1 Kings 12RehoboamJeroboam

The kingdom divides. Ten tribes north (Israel), two south (Judah).

860 BC1 Kings 18ElijahAhabJezebel

Elijah on Carmel. Fire answers by fire; the still small voice follows at Horeb.

722 BC2 Kings 17

Fall of the northern kingdom. Assyria deports the ten tribes — the "lost tribes" whose scattering haunts all three volumes.

700 BCIsaiah 6IsaiahHezekiah

Isaiah's ministry. The prophet whose words travel to the New World on the plates of brass.

621 BC2 Kings 22Josiah

Josiah's reform. The found book read aloud — one generation before Lehi's call.

586 BC2 Kings 25JeremiahNebuchadnezzar

Jerusalem falls to Babylon. The destruction Lehi prophesied and fled; Mulek's escape party also departs (per Helaman 8:21).

550 BCDaniel 2DanielEzekiel

Daniel in Babylon. Exile prophecy: the image with feet of clay, the valley of dry bones.

516 BCEzra 6

Second temple completed. The return under Cyrus's decree bears fruit.

445 BCNehemiah 8EzraNehemiah

Ezra & Nehemiah. Walls rebuilt in fifty-two days; the law read to a weeping assembly.

430 BCMalachi 4Malachi

Malachi closes the Hebrew canon. Elijah's promised return — the words the risen Christ later dictates at Bountiful.

4 BCLuke 2Jesus ChristMaryJoseph of Nazareth

Birth of Jesus Christ. Bethlehem; the sign appears simultaneously in the New World (3 Nephi 1).

AD 27Matthew 3John the Baptist

Baptism & ministry begins. Jordan, wilderness, Galilee.

AD 30Matthew 5The Twelve Apostles

Sermon on the Mount. The sermon redelivered, with covenant reframings, at Bountiful four years later.

AD 33John 19Jesus ChristMary MagdalenePontius Pilate

Crucifixion & Resurrection. Three hours of darkness in Judea; three days of darkness in the New World.

AD 34Acts 2Peter

Pentecost & the church. Three thousand baptized; the apostolic age opens.

AD 35Acts 9Paul

Conversion of Paul. The Damascus road — the Old World's Alma₂ moment.

AD 50Acts 15JamesPeterPaul

Jerusalem council. The Gentile question settled; the mission goes to the nations.

AD 95Revelation 1John the Beloved

Revelation on Patmos. The apostle Nephi₁ saw in vision writes "the remainder of these things."

New World

2200 BCEther 1JaredThe Brother of JaredThe Jaredites

Jaredite departure from Babel. Language preserved, barges built; "the Lord did go before them."

2150 BCEther 3The Brother of JaredJesus Christ

The Lord appears to the brother of Jared. The premortal Christ unveiled on the mount — the canon's earliest full theophany of the Son.

1200 BCEther 8Akish

Jaredite kingdom cycles. Prosperity, captivity, secret combinations — centuries compressed into Moroni's abridgment.

600 BC1 Nephi 2Lehi₁SariahNephi₁Laman₁

Lehi leaves Jerusalem. First year of Zedekiah: the family flees before the destruction Lehi prophesied.

597 BC1 Nephi 11Nephi₁

Nephi's vision of the tree. The condescension of God: Mary, the Baptist, the Twelve, and John — seen six centuries early.

590 BCEther 15EtherCoriantumr₂

Jaredite destruction at Ramah. The last battle; Coriantumr alone survives to be found by the people of Zarahemla.

589 BC1 Nephi 18Nephi₁Lehi₁

Arrival in the promised land. Eight years in Arabia, then the ship Nephi was shown how to build.

570 BC2 Nephi 5Nephi₁Laman₁The NephitesThe Lamanites

Nephite–Lamanite division. After Lehi's death the family fractures permanently into two nations.

545 BC2 Nephi 9Jacob₂

Jacob's temple discourses. The atonement discourse and, later, Zenos' olive-tree allegory.

420 BCEnos 1Enos

Enos's wrestle. A whole generation's faith distilled into one all-day prayer.

200 BCOmni 1Mosiah₁ZarahemlaThe People of Zarahemla

Mosiah₁ discovers Zarahemla. Two Jerusalem exoduses meet; the Mulekites take Mosiah as king.

148 BCMosiah 13AbinadiKing NoahAlma₁

Abinadi before King Noah. Isaiah 53 recited in chains; one priest believes.

124 BCMosiah 2King BenjaminMosiah₂

King Benjamin's address. The tower sermon: a covenant people take the name of Christ.

100 BCMosiah 27Alma₂Alma₁Ammon₂

Alma₂ & the angel. The persecutor struck down — the New World's Damascus road, a century early.

91 BCMosiah 29Mosiah₂Alma₂

Reign of the judges begins. Mosiah₂ abolishes kingship; Alma₂ is first chief judge.

90 BCAlma 17Ammon₂Aaron₃LamoniLamoni's Father

Mission to the Lamanites. Fourteen years among enemies; thousands convert and bury their swords.

74 BCAlma 46Moroni₁Amalickiah

Title of liberty. The rent coat raised: "in memory of our God, our religion, and freedom."

64 BCAlma 57Helaman₂The Two Thousand Stripling Warriors

The two thousand at Cumeni. Every one wounded; not one lost — "they did not doubt."

52 BCHelaman 2GadiantonKishkumen

Gadianton's band forms. The secret combination Mormon names as the nation's eventual overthrow.

20 BCHelaman 10Nephi₂

Nephi₂ receives the sealing power. "All things shall be done unto thee according to thy word" — he asks for famine instead of war.

6 BCHelaman 14Samuel the Lamanite

Samuel on the wall. The five-year sign of the birth; the arrows cannot touch him.

AD 13 Nephi 1Nephi₃

The sign of Christ's birth. Night without darkness, on the eve of the believers' execution.

AD 343 Nephi 8

Destruction & three days of darkness. Cities sink and burn at the crucifixion; a voice in the dark names the Redeemer.

AD 34.53 Nephi 11Jesus ChristNephi₃The Twelve Disciples

Christ at the temple in Bountiful. 2,500 people feel the wounds one by one; the Sermon redelivered; the sacrament instituted.

AD 363 Nephi 28The Three Nephites

The Three Nephites tarry. John's wish granted again, an ocean away.

AD 1004 Nephi 1

Zion society. "There could not be a happier people…" — two centuries without contention, then the slow unravelling.

AD 322Mormon 1AmmaronMormon

Mormon receives the records. The archive's future entrusted to a sober child of ten.

AD 385Mormon 6MormonMoroni₂

The last battle at Cumorah. "O ye fair ones" — the Nephite nation ends; Mormon falls with it.

AD 421Moroni 10Moroni₂

Moroni seals the record. Alone for decades, he closes with a promise addressed to the future reader — then buries the plates.

The First Vision. James 1:5 sends a fourteen-year-old into the grove; his history records the Father and the Son in answer.

AD 1823Joseph Smith--History 1Joseph Smith Jr.Moroni₂

Moroni's night visits. The Book of Mormon's final historian, fourteen centuries on, appears three times in one night quoting Malachi, Isaiah, and Joel.

AD 1827Joseph Smith--History 1Joseph Smith Jr.Moroni₂

The plates entrusted. After four annual visits to the hill, the record Moroni buried changes hands.

AD 1829Joseph Smith--History 1Joseph Smith Jr.John the Baptist

Baptismal authority restored. The history's account of John the Baptist — forerunner twice over — on the banks of the Susquehanna.

AD 1830Joseph Smith--History 1Joseph Smith Jr.

The Book of Mormon published. Mormon's abridgment reaches its addressed reader: "it shall come forth by way of the Gentile."
