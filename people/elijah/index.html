Elijah

the Tishbite

Fire on Carmel, stillness at Horeb, chariot at Jordan. Malachi's closing promise of his return frames both Testaments — and is quoted by Christ to the Nephites.

Key passages: 1 Kings 18 · 1 Kings 19 · 2 Kings 2
