Laman₁

eldest son of Lehi₁, namesake of the Lamanites

Murmurer, mutineer, and angel-rebuked elder brother whose grievance hardens into a nation's name and a millennium of division.

Key passages: 1 Nephi 2 · 1 Nephi 17 · 2 Nephi 5

On the timeline

600 BC1 Nephi 2

Lehi leaves Jerusalem. First year of Zedekiah: the family flees before the destruction Lehi prophesied.

570 BC2 Nephi 5

Nephite–Lamanite division. After Lehi's death the family fractures permanently into two nations.
