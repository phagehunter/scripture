Peoples and movements · Old Testament

The Children of Israel

the covenant people of the Exodus and after

The nation delivered through the sea, covenanted at Sinai, and endlessly wandering between fidelity and forgetting.

A collective node standing for about 600,000 people.

Key passages: Exodus 14 · Exodus 19
