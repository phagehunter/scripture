Samuel the Lamanite

prophet on the wall of Zarahemla (not Samuel of Israel)

The outsider prophet the arrows cannot touch: gives the five-year sign of Christ's birth and the three days of darkness — the only Lamanite whose words the risen Christ personally orders added to the record.

Key passages: Helaman 13 · Helaman 14 · 3 Nephi 23
