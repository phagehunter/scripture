Jacob₂

son of Lehi₁, first wilderness-born — not the patriarch Israel

Consecrated priest by his brother; preacher of the atonement, denouncer of pride and polygamy, and canonizer of Zenos' olive allegory. Dies asking, "our lives passed away like as it were unto us a dream."

Key passages: 2 Nephi 9 · Jacob 2 · Jacob 5
