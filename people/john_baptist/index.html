John the Baptist

the forerunner

Voice in the wilderness foreseen by Lehi and Nephi₁ by name of office — camel's hair, locusts, and the baptism of the One whose shoe-latchet he felt unworthy to loose.

Key passages: Matthew 3 · John 1 · 1 Nephi 10
