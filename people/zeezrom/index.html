Zeezrom

lawyer of Ammonihah, converted accuser

Offers six onties of silver to deny God, is caught in his own cross-examination, burns with fever until healed by the men he prosecuted — then preaches beside them.

Key passages: Alma 11 · Alma 14 · Alma 15
