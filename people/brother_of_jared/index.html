The Brother of Jared

Mahonri, the great Jaredite seer

The seer whose faith is so complete that the premortal Lord cannot stay hidden: he sees the finger, then the whole spiritual body, of Christ on the mount.

Key passages: Ether 3 · Ether 6

On the timeline

2200 BCEther 1

Jaredite departure from Babel. Language preserved, barges built; "the Lord did go before them."

2150 BCEther 3

The Lord appears to the brother of Jared. The premortal Christ unveiled on the mount — the canon's earliest full theophany of the Son.

familyweight 4Ether 1:34

Jared and The Brother of Jared. Brothers at the great tower; one asks, the other pleads with the Lord.

divine encounterweight 5Ether 3:9-14

Jesus Christ and The Brother of Jared. The premortal Christ shows himself: "never has man come before me with such exceeding faith… behold, I am Jesus Christ."

Moroni₂ and The Brother of Jared. Abridging Ether, Moroni withholds the full vision "until the Gentiles shall repent" — editor and seer across two millennia.
