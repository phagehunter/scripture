Rahab

of Jericho, who hid the spies

The Canaanite woman whose scarlet cord saves her household when the walls fall — grafted into the royal genealogy as mother of Boaz, and cited by name in the New Testament's rolls of faith.

Key passages: Joshua 2 · Joshua 6 · Hebrews 11
