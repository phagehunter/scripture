Paul

Saul of Tarsus, apostle to the Gentiles

Persecutor blinded into apostleship on the Damascus road — his conversion narrative and Alma₂'s are the canon's twin dramas of the arrested persecutor.

Key passages: Acts 9 · Romans 1 · 1 Corinthians 13

On the timeline

AD 35Acts 9

Conversion of Paul. The Damascus road — the Old World's Alma₂ moment.

AD 50Acts 15

Jerusalem council. The Gentile question settled; the mission goes to the nations.

Relationships (14)

conflictweight 3Acts 7:58

Paul and Stephen. The witnesses lay their clothes at Saul's feet; he is "consenting unto his death."

divine encounterweight 5Acts 9:3-6

Jesus Christ and Paul. The Damascus road: "Saul, Saul, why persecutest thou me?" — the persecutor blinded into apostleship.

allianceweight 4Acts 9:26-27

Barnabas and Paul. Vouches for the feared convert at Jerusalem and recruits him to Antioch — the partnership of the first missions.

teaching and conversionweight 41 Timothy 1:2

Paul and Timothy. "My own son in the faith" — heir of the Gentile mission's pastoral charge.

conflictweight 3Galatians 2:11-14

Paul and Peter. Antioch confrontation "to the face" over table fellowship — apostles in open disagreement.

allianceweight 32 Timothy 4:11

Luke and Paul. "Only Luke is with me" — companion of the voyages and the last imprisonment.

teaching and conversionweight 4Acts 9:10-18

Ananias and Paul. The reluctant healer of the persecutor: "Brother Saul, the Lord… hath sent me, that thou mightest receive thy sight."

teaching and conversionweight 3Acts 16:13-15

Paul and Lydia. By the river at Philippi, "whose heart the Lord opened" — Europe's first convert and first host.

allianceweight 4Acts 16:25-34

Paul and Silas. Chosen for the second journey; hymns at midnight in the Philippian stocks until the doors fly open.

allianceweight 3Romans 16:3-4

Paul and Priscilla. Same craft, shared roof, and a house-church greeting: "my helpers in Christ Jesus, who have for my life laid down their own necks."

allianceweight 2Acts 18:1-3

Paul and Aquila. Lodging and livelihood at Corinth: "because he was of the same craft, he abode with them."

allianceweight 31 Corinthians 3:4-6

Paul and Apollos. Rival banners at Corinth resolved: "I have planted, Apollos watered; but God gave the increase."

teaching and conversionweight 3Titus 1:4-5

Paul and Titus. "Mine own son after the common faith" — envoy to Corinth, steward of the collection, organizer of Crete.

teaching and conversionweight 3Philemon 1:10-17

Paul and Philemon. The letter for Onesimus: "receive him… not now as a servant, but above a servant, a brother beloved."
