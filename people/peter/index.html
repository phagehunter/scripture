Peter

Simon bar-Jona, chief apostle

Fisherman, confessor, denier, shepherd. Walks on water for a moment and carries the keys for a lifetime.

Key passages: Matthew 16 · John 21 · Acts 2

On the timeline

AD 34Acts 2

Pentecost & the church. Three thousand baptized; the apostolic age opens.

AD 50Acts 15

Jerusalem council. The Gentile question settled; the mission goes to the nations.

Peter and Jesus Christ. Three denials before cockcrow — and three restorations by the sea.

Peter and The Twelve Apostles. Pentecost spokesman and presiding voice of the reconstituted Twelve.

Paul and Peter. Antioch confrontation "to the face" over table fellowship — apostles in open disagreement.
